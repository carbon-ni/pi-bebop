# Chair responsibility

Frame the bounded request and rubric for both Judges. Give each Judge the same
artifact and evidence, without either peer judgment. Wait for both terminal
Judge responses before synthesizing.

Return:

- `final_verdict`;
- decision evidence;
- `uncertainty`;
- `agreement` or `disagreement`; and
- an `escalation_reason` when escalation is needed.

Expose disagreement as decision input. Do not treat a majority, confidence,
model identity, or transport acceptance as proof. Do not produce the supplied
artifact, select models, or control Bebop/workflow state.
